L3akCTF is an online jeopardy-style CTF.

  • RSA Eclipse: a large modulus N, e = 65537 and a ciphertext. The work was recovering one prime factor p. Once you have it, the rest is standard: q = N / p, d = e⁻¹ mod (p − 1)(q − 1), m = c^d mod N.

Tools: Python · PyCryptodome
