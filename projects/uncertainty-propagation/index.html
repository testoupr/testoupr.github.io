Generalized Uncertainty Propagation

CompletePHYS 159February 2025GitHub ↗

A MATLAB function for PHYS 159 labs that propagates measurement uncertainty through any formula in one call, instead of redoing the derivatives by hand each time.

δy=∑i(∂y∂xi δxi)2\delta y = \sqrt{\sum_i \left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial x_i}\, \delta x_i\right)^2}δy=i∑​(∂xi​∂y​δxi​)2​

It builds the Jacobian with the Symbolic Math Toolbox, evaluates it at your measurements, and combines each partial derivative with its uncertainty.

Stack: MATLAB Live Script · Symbolic Math Toolbox
